## 1. Project Overview & Motivation

Reliable multi-step forecasting of electricity consumption and photovoltaic (PV) solar generation is critical for smart grid stability, renewable integration, and demand-side management. In this project, under the strict condition of using only historical univariate/multivariate target observations (without external exogenous weather or calendar variables), we design, implement, and benchmark a complete taxonomy of time-series models:

1. **Non-Deep Learning Baselines:**
   - **Persistence (Last Observation):** Naive propagation of $x[t-1]$ over the entire 24-hour horizon.
   - **Daily Seasonal Naive:** Propagating the exact corresponding 24-hour segment from the previous day ($t - m$).
2. **Foundational Deep Learning:**
   - **LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory):** Gated recurrent memory cell with hidden state direct projection head.
   - **GRU (Gated Recurrent Unit):** Streamlined recurrent cell with update and reset gates.
   - **1D-CNN:** Multi-scale temporal convolutional feature extractor with adaptive pooling.
3. **Hybrid Architectures:**
   - **CNN-LSTM:** Serial combination exploiting 1D-CNN for local sub-pattern extraction followed by LSTM for long-range temporal dependency encoding.
4. **Modern Time-Series Architectures (Time-Series-Library):**
   - **DLinear (AAAI 2023):** Moving-average trend-seasonal decomposition combined with dual linear projection branches.
   - **PatchTST (ICLR 2023):** Channel-independent tokenization via temporal patching and Transformer self-attention.

## 3. Strict Machine Learning Protocols & Best Practices

In accordance with `/ml-best-practices` and the project outline:

1. **Chronological Data Partitioning (No Random Splits):**
   - Given total sequence length $T$:
     - Train interval: $[0, b_1)$ where $b_1 = \lfloor 0.70 \times T \rfloor$.
     - Validation interval: $[b_1, b_2)$ where $b_2 = \lfloor 0.80 \times T \rfloor$.
     - Test interval: $[b_2, T)$.
   - Target windows $y[t:t+H]$ are strictly confined to their respective interval. Past observations $x[t-L:t]$ may cross the boundary from earlier intervals because they represent already observed historical context.
2. **Strict Featurization Ordering (No Data Leakage):**
   - The `StandardScaler` calculates mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$ **exclusively** on the training target slice $[0, b_1)$.
   - Zero-variance channels are protected with $\max(\sigma, 10^{-8})$.
   - The validation and test splits are transformed using the fitted training statistics.
   - All evaluation metrics are calculated on the **inverse-transformed original scale**.
3. **Seasonal MASE (Mean Absolute Scaled Error):**
   - Evaluated against a daily seasonal benchmark with period $m$ ($m=24$ for Electricity, $m=144$ for Solar):
     $$\text{MASE} = \frac{\text{MAE}}{\frac{1}{T_{\text{train}} - m} \sum_{t=m+1}^{T_{\text{train}}} |y_t - y_{t-m}|}$$
   - The denominator is strictly calculated on the training interval.
4. **Computational Profiling:**
   - Trainable parameter counts, training wall-clock time, GPU/CPU memory, and batch inference latency (ms/batch with CUDA warmup) are measured and logged to `experiments.db`.

## 6. Sensitivity & Ablation Analysis

- **Experiment A: Historical Window Length Sensitivity:**
  Evaluates $L \in \{24, 48, 168\}$ hours for Electricity and $L \in \{144, 288, 1008\}$ steps for Solar on LSTM and PatchTST to analyze whether longer history yields diminished returns or overfitting.
- **Experiment B: Training Data Volume Sensitivity:**
  Evaluates 25%, 50%, and 100% of historical training data ending at $b_1$, determining sample efficiency.
- **Experiment C: Modular CNN Ablation:**
  Directly contrasts CNN-LSTM against pure LSTM and pure 1D-CNN to quantify the marginal performance benefit vs. parameter complexity of convolutional local feature extraction.
